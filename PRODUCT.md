## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are B2B decision-makers in Nigeria's maritime sector: vessel owners/operators, shipping and offshore companies, and technical/fleet managers responsible for keeping diesel-powered vessels and marine equipment operational. They arrive needing either urgent repair (unplanned engine downtime) or planned technical support (maintenance contracts, consultancy, staffing, procurement). Based in and primarily serving Port Harcourt, Rivers State, a major Nigerian oil & gas / marine hub.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website for Umendu Marine Engineering Services Limited that establishes credibility with technical buyers, communicates the full service range, and converts visitors into quote requests or direct engineer contact (call/WhatsApp). Success is a qualified inquiry (quote request, consultation booking, or contact form/WhatsApp message).

## Positioning

A single marine engineering partner covering the full lifecycle of vessel technical needs, diesel engine maintenance/repair across all major global brands, engineering consultancy and general contracts, plus the adjacent operational support (logistics, procurement, technician staffing, vessel hire) that competitors typically split across multiple vendors. Diagnoses root cause rather than treating symptoms.

## Operating Context

Maritime/marine engineering industry in Nigeria. Client vessels run diesel engines from Caterpillar, Cummins, MAK, Niigata, Yanmar, Daihatsu, MAN B&W, and Wärtsilä. Downtime is costly for clients, so response speed and technical precision are core to the value proposition. Work spans routine scheduled servicing to emergency call-outs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three service pillars (each needs its own page per copy.md):

1. **Maintenance & Repairs** — routine maintenance, full engine overhauls, emergency repairs, diagnostics & performance checks, spare parts replacement, across the diesel brands listed above.
2. **Engineering Services** — ET diagnosis & troubleshooting, technical consultancy, general contracts, ship general maintenance. Follows a 4-step approach: Assessment, Diagnosis, Execution, Follow-Up.
3. **Other Services** — maritime logistics, supply of ship engine parts, supply of competent technicians, vessel hiring & leasing, marine procurement.

## Product Principles

- Technical credibility first: the design must read as competent and serious to an engineering/procurement audience, not consumer-marketing slick.
- Speed-to-contact: every page keeps a fast path to quote request, call, or WhatsApp, since downtime urgency is the core buying trigger.
- One-partner breadth without dilution: the multi-service range (engineering + logistics + procurement + staffing) must feel unified, not like a bolted-together services list.
- Root-cause expertise over generic reassurance: favor concrete, specific technical language over vague trust-badge marketing filler.
- Safety and compliance as a stated value, not just a slogan.
